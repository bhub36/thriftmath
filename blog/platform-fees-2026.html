What Every Reselling Platform Actually Charges (Mid-2026)

ThriftMath Guides

Fees are where reselling profit quietly dies — and they change constantly (TikTok Shop has changed its rate three times since launch). Here's the mid-2026 landscape; always confirm current rates in your seller dashboard.

What the table doesn't show

  • Poshmark's 20% includes the shipping label — for heavy items it can beat "cheaper" platforms.
  • Facebook local is the margin king — zero fees, but your costs are gas and time (track the mileage; it's deductible).
  • TikTok Shop's 6% is before affiliate commissions — offer a creator 13% and your real take is ~19% off the top, plus sample costs.

The point
